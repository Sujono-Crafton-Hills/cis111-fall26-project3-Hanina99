The Song That Changed Everything

Wildly regarded as the song that broke King Gnu into the mainstream, "Hakujitsu" was released digitally on February 22, 2019, shortly after King Gnu made their major-label debut with the album Sympa. It was written as the theme song for the Japanese TV drama Innocence: False Accusation Lawyer.

The song became a massive breakthrough for the band. In 2019, its music video reached 100 million YouTube views in about nine and a half months, and it ranked #4 on Billboard Japan's year-end Japan Hot 100. It was also the #2 trending music video in Japan on YouTube for 2019.

What made it especially important for King Gnu was that it introduced them to a much wider audience. Their dramatic vocals, emotional melody, and mixture of rock, pop, classical elements, and electronic sounds made the song stand out. Contemporary analysis of their 2019 breakout specifically identifies "Hakujitsu" as the song that greatly increased their public recognition, while appearances in TV, commercials, and streaming platforms helped that popularity spread even further. And its popularity didn't disappear after 2019. As of September 2026, "Hakujitsu" has surpassed 700 million cumulative streams in Japan, making it King Gnu's first song to reach that milestone. Its YouTube music video had already passed 500 million views by November 2024.

King Gnu is a Japanese band formed in 2013 specializing in rock, J-Pop, indie pop, and pop rock. It consists of Daiki Tsuneta (vocalist and guitarist), Satoru Iguchi (vocalist and keyboardist), Kazuki Arai (bassist) and Yu Seki (drummer). The band's main songwriter is Daiki Tsuneta, who also performs as part of the creative collective Millennium Parade. Although they were originally named Mrs. Vinci, they renamed themselves "King Gnu" in 2017 and later released their first album under the name King Gnu, "Tokyo Rendez-Vous." This was the beginning of their rise to fame, and they have since released several albums and singles, gaining a large following in Japan as well as internationally.

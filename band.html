Band Members

King Gnu is a four-member band consisting of Daiki Tsuneta (vocalist and guitarist), Satoru Iguchi (vocalist and keyboardist), Kazuki Arai (bassist), and Yu Seki (drummer). The band was formed in 2013 under the name Mrs. Vinci, but they changed their name to King Gnu in 2017. They have since gained popularity in Japan and internationally for their unique sound, which blends rock, pop, classical elements, and electronic music.

Satoru Iguchi

Iguchi is a major vocalist in King Gnu, alongside Tsuneta. His voice provides a striking contrast to Tsuneta's: Iguchi can move between soft, delicate singing and much more powerful high-register vocals. He studied vocal music at Tokyo University of the Arts, the same university Tsuneta attended. Tsuneta and Iguchi actually knew each other from childhood and attended the same schools in Nagano. Iguchi has also worked outside music. He's appeared as an actor and television narrator, and previously hosted his own radio program, King Gnu Iguchi Satoru's All Night Nippon 0.
  • Main vocalist and keyboardist
  • Actor outside of the band

Daiki Tsuneta

Tsuneta began learning cello at age 5 and later attended the Tokyo University of the Arts, studying cello. He eventually left the university because he wanted to make music connected more broadly with contemporary culture and society. Before King Gnu, he started a project called Srv.Vinci in 2013. After several lineup changes, the group evolved into King Gnu, adopting that name in 2017. Tsuneta is also heavily involved in the creative collective PERIMETRON and leads MILLENNIUM PARADE, a separate musical project. His work extends beyond performing into songwriting, production, visual direction, and overall artistic design.
  • Vocalist and guitarist
  • Leader of the band

Yu Seki

Seki comes from a family of professional musicians, and he started playing drums when he was young. He also studied dance as a child, which is an interesting part of his background because his musical performance is strongly connected to rhythm and movement. His role in King Gnu isn't simply traditional rock drumming. The official profile specifically lists him as “Drs./Sampler,” reflecting the band's combination of live instrumentation and electronic sounds.
  • Drummer and sampler
  • Married to Ki&Ki's shamisen player Shirafuji Hikari

Kazuki Arai

Arai began playing bass at 14 and later studied at Tokyo Keizai University. Before King Gnu became internationally known, he was active in the jazz scene and was a member of the NEWTIDE JAZZ ORCHESTRA. The group won the highest award at the Yamano Big Band Jazz Contest. His jazz background is particularly relevant to King Gnu because the band's bass lines often aren't just straightforward rock bass. They can be funky, jazzy, melodic, or highly rhythmic. Arai also has a signature Fender bass, the Deluxe Jazz Bass V Kazuki Arai Edition, released alongside Tsuneta's signature guitar.
  • Bassist
  • Studied under jazz bassist Osamu Kawakami
